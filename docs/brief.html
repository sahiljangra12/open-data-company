Daily brief

Each day, after the data is refreshed, the Data Agent reads what changed, checks the newest figures in the datasets and writes this short note. It runs on GitHub, with tools written for this site.

26 November 2026, 20:25 UTC

A powerful earthquake struck near Indonesia and Tonga today

What changed

  • A 5.7-magnitude earthquake hit the Revilla Gigedo Islands region at 15:26 UTC on 26 November 2026. Source: USGS.
  • The highest recorded magnitude in the past 30 days was 5.9, on 25 November at 01:38 UTC. Source: USGS Earthquake Hazards Program.
  • Daily calorie supply in Albania (2024) was 3,348; in Angola, 2,365; in Afghanistan, 2,310. Source: Food supply dataset.
  • Senegal’s goods trade balance improved from -2.011 billion to -1.973 billion USD in 2025. Source: World trade in goods (UN Comtrade).

Worth a look

  • Explore biodiversity-records-by-year to see how species are being observed globally.
  • Check earthquakes-past-30-days for a full timeline of seismic events.

Written by qwen3:4b-instruct, an open model that runs on GitHub's own machines during the run (no outside AI service). Tools it called: get_series, get_values, earthquakes_live. Before publishing, each figure in the note is matched against what the tools returned; a note that fails is replaced by a plain summary of the change log. A small model can still word things loosely, so check a figure in the datasets or the change log before you cite it.

Ask the agent yourself

The same agent answers questions posted as GitHub issues, usually within about ten minutes, with the figures, their years and their sources. For instant answers in the browser, use Data AI.
