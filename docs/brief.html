Daily brief

Each day, after the data is refreshed, the Data Agent reads what changed, checks the newest figures in the datasets and writes this short note. It runs on GitHub, with tools written for this site.

26 November 2026, 21:27 UTC

Africa’s daily calorie supply in 2024 is 2568

What changed

  • Africa’s daily calorie supply per person is 2568 in 2024 (unit: calories), from FAO data (2024), source: food-supply dataset.
  • The strongest earthquake of the past day was 5.7 magnitude near Revilla Gigedo Islands region at 2026-11-26 15:26 UTC, per USGS.
  • The global estimate of armed conflict deaths in 2025 is 108,907 in Africa (unit: deaths), from UCDP data (2025), source: conflict-and-security dataset.

Worth a look

  • Explore food-supply for daily calorie and meat per person by region, updated to 2024.
  • Check democracy-and-rights for electoral democracy index (V-Dem) trends from 1789 to 2025.

Written by qwen3:4b-instruct, an open model that runs on GitHub's own machines during the run (no outside AI service). Tools it called: find_variables, earthquakes_live. Before publishing, each figure in the note is matched against what the tools returned; a note that fails is replaced by a plain summary of the change log. A small model can still word things loosely, so check a figure in the datasets or the change log before you cite it.
