Ask the data

Type what you want to know. You get the number, its year and its source, read straight from the datasets, with a chart and a citation you can paste. Nothing is guessed or written by a model.

Try

What you can ask

One number

A measure and a place: Kenya internet users, Japan population 1990. You get the value, how it ranks, how it has moved in ten years, and the full history.

A comparison

A measure and several places: Germany vs France vs Spain renewable electricity. You get the values side by side and one chart with a line for each.

A ranking

Words like top, highest or lowest: lowest child mortality, top 5 wind power. You get the list, and each name leads to that country's own answer.

A world series

Oil price, global temperature, CO2 in the atmosphere, earthquakes today. You get the latest reading, the change in a year and the whole record.

Why not a search engine or a chatbot? A search gives you ten links with ten different figures to reconcile. A chatbot gives you one figure with no way to check it. Here every answer names its dataset, its year and its licence, and one click opens the rows it came from. The limit is honest too: it only knows what is in the datasets listed here.
