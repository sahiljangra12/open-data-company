    AI

    Hi, I'm Data AI. Ask me anything, by typing or with the microphone. I answer with figures from the open datasets on this site, each with its year and its source, and I can draw a chart or a map when you ask for one.

    Figures are read from the datasets. Explanations are written by an AI model and can be wrong, so check what matters. Enter sends, Shift and Enter starts a new line.

    What you can ask

    One number

    A measure and a place: Kenya internet users, Japan population 1990, US unemployment rate, India population in 2050. You get the value, how it ranks, how it moved and the full history.

    A comparison or a ranking

    Germany vs France vs Spain renewable electricity, happiest countries, least corrupt countries, top 5 wind power. Values side by side, or the list of leaders.

    A chart or a map

    Chart India's CO2 emissions since 1990, map of democracy, chart the US national debt, plot life expectancy against GDP per capita. Drawn in the chat, with a picture and the numbers to download.

    Why and how

    Why did oil prices jump in 2022? Is Africa getting more electricity? The AI reads the figures first, then explains them in plain words. A topic outside the data still gets an answer, labelled as general knowledge.

    States, colleges, foods

    Rent in Texas against California, which US colleges cost the most, protein in lentils, the latest US bank failures. The AI reads the rows from the table and shows which ones it used.

    How it answers

    1. Plain lookups and charts are instant. A request such as India life expectancy or chart oil price since 2020 is matched to a variable and read straight from the data. No AI is involved, so the numbers cannot be invented.
    2. Everything else goes to an AI model. When it needs a figure or a chart it asks this page for it, and the page reads the values from the same datasets. "Data behind this answer" under a reply opens exactly what was read. The answer appears while it is being written, and the Stop button ends it early.
    3. Two models can write. The hosted Claude model answers by default. Under "Who answers" you can also download a small open model that runs in your own browser: it takes over when the hosted one is resting, or answers alone if you choose "This device only". Every figure the small model writes is matched against the data it read before you see it.
    4. Your chats are kept for you. Each chat is saved in this browser, listed at the side, and can be opened again, carried on, renamed or deleted. Nothing is stored on a server.
    5. Each answer leads on. Under a reply are two or three questions you could ask next. Press one to ask it.
    6. General knowledge is labelled. If the datasets hold nothing on a topic, the AI answers from what it knows and says so. Like any AI it can be wrong or out of date there, so check anything that matters.

    Data AI is free to use. Questions for the hosted AI are sent to a Claude model through this site's small service and are not stored with your name; questions answered by the on-device model stay on your device. Voice input uses your browser's speech recognition, which in some browsers sends the audio to the browser's maker. Please do not include personal details. The AI has a daily allowance; when it is used up, answers and charts come straight from the datasets until the next day.
